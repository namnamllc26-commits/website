Behind the Box

How It Started

After years working in the grueling restaurant industry in Chicago, Chef Martin immigrated back home to Cebu. He was seeking a much-needed change of pace. He still loved baking and found immense comfort in it, but he wanted to leave the exhausting kitchen hours behind.

It was his best friend, Patricia, who sparked the idea that would change everything.

"Let's do a microbakery," she suggested. "I noticed that most bread shops are going Western, and the Philippines has a rich history of breads too. I'm afraid it'll get lost in time and the kids won't be able to connect. Let's do something about it. It'll be fun."

Martin agreed to the "fun" little project to keep his hands in the dough while honoring their shared heritage.

Well, it was fun... and then it completely got out of hand.

Word spread, the ovens couldn't keep up, and that tiny microbakery blossomed into BreadBox Cebu. Today, we are proud to be your Modern Panaderia, dedicated to preserving Filipino bread culture with world-class technique and a whole lot of love.
